🍞 Community & Starvation

Every 10 lines cleared = 1 game day passes.

Your community needs 1,500 cal per person per day to survive. Calories are earned when full rows are cleared.

A calorie bar shows your surplus or deficit. Fall more than 2 days behind and your community starts starving. Keep pace or the hunt fails!

Bigger animals spawn rarely – prioritise clearing rows over chasing a specific piece.
